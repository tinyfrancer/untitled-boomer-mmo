For this feature we want to focus on enemies.

Enemies should have levels, and these levels should dictate their base stats.
Example (Not necessarily real stats): 
    - Lvl 1 rat - 10 health , 1 strength
    - Lvl 2 rat - 12 health , 2 strength 

In our starting area, the rats can be between level 1 and 3.  There should be more level 1 spawns than level 2, and less level 3 spawns

Higher level enemies should hit harder, be harder to kill, and provide better exp.  Drop rates can remain the same for now.

Enemy names should change color based on relative level to the character. Their level should also be displayed by the name.
Example:
    - Green same level 
    - Yellow 1-2 levels above 
    - Red 3 + 


Combat:

Enemies should retaliate and attack player when attacked.  Rats will not attack first, but some enemies might.
When the player dies, for now they will just respawn back at the main spawn. 
Enemies should regenerate health when player is killed.

Ask any questions about additional features that should be added, or suggestions or requirements.
