Spells/Skills:
We want our characters to have spells and skills. 
Mage should have Fireball and Mana Shield
Warrior should have a power slash and maybe something to boost attack speed for a few seconds.
Very basic but we want to set the groundwork for expansion.  

Alongside this we should have attribute/skills related to the spells.
Fireball could be from the destruction family of spells. Destruction should be a levelable stat. Higher levels should slightly increase spell damage, and reduce chance of spell failure
Spells can fail to cast.

There should be stats for things like 1 handed weapons, fist damage, block, parry, etc.  I will let you decide on the base implementation and we can iterate on it.

Let's go with the 10x approach of things like 1 handed max level being 10x current level.
